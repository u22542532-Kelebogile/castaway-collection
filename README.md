# 🏝️ The Castaway Collection
### Our Digital Time Capsule · INL 340 (2026)

If you were stranded on a desert island, what would you take with you?
This repository is our answer: a digital time capsule built by seven INL 340 students.

## What's inside
- 🎵 **7 songs**, each with the story of why we'd take it
- 📜 **21 digitised artefacts**, with original and edited versions
- 💾 **14 born-digital items** and what they mean to us

Every item is described with metadata (title, description, format, dates, owner, copyright) and can be searched and browsed on the site.
